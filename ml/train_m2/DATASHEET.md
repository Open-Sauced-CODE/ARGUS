# Datasheet for ARGUS M2 Training and Calibration Corpus

## 1. Motivation

- **Purpose:** Provide empirical training, calibration, and validation data for the ARGUS Trust Engine (M2). M2 evaluates whether gate-passing Patch-IR remediation patches generated by local language models may safely auto-apply without manual human review.
- **Creators:** ARGUS ML-systems team (Phase 6).
- **Primary Consumers:** M2 training pipeline, Learn-Then-Test (LTT) risk calibration, and Phase 7 runtime decision engine.

- **Input Candidates (`ml/train_m2/contract/harvest_inputs.jsonl`):**
  - Total unique inputs: 321 distinct input clusters (`input_key`).
  - Origin: Projected through the Phase 4 privacy airlock across CIS Benchmark and DPDP rules (AWS IAM, S3, RDS, EC2).

## 5. Label Construction

- **Ground-Truth Target:** `strict_minimal_correct_v1` (strict oracle).
- **Oracle Pipeline (`ml/train_m2/labels/oracle.py`):**
  - Evaluates Rego QA gate passage (`gate_pass`), patch outcome (`fixed`, `escalated`, `rejected`), and four hidden verification checks:
    1. `full_equivalence`: Patch resolves the violation rule.
    2. `minimal_patch`: Patch contains no unnecessary ops beyond the minimal fix.
    3. `decoys_untouched`: Decoy attributes remain untouched.
    4. `no_side_effects`: No collateral policy regressions introduced.
  - Seven oracle classes:
    - `minimal_correct`: Target y = 1.
    - `valid_not_minimal`: Gate passes, harmless extra edits; counted as strict negative (y = 0) but tracked separately.
    - `wrong`: Harmful error (gate failed or introduced regressions).
    - `escalated_ok`: Valid escalation on escalate route (excluded from M2 training).
    - `escalated_unneeded`: Unnecessary escalation on fix route.
    - `fixed_should_escalate`: Harmful fix accepted by gate on escalate-route input.
    - `unknown`: Gate-passing patch with unresolvable reference.
- **Counterfactual Labelling:**
  - Evaluates patches lacking human reference via synthetic counterfactual mutation.
  - Assessed as weak (recall 0.926, precision 0.858 against known corruptions; 22% false negatives); excluded from evaluation.
- **Corruptor Hard Negatives (`ml/train_m2/out/labels/corruptor_train.jsonl`):**
  - 144 distinct hard negatives created across types C1-C6 using train-only seeds.
  - Restricted strictly to the training split; never present in validation, calibration, or held-out sets.

## 6. Known Biases & Data Limitations

1. **Extreme Class Imbalance on SFT:**
   Production SFT achieves 96.7% pass@1 on seen inputs. Natural incorrect completions are rare on SFT (only 3 negative clusters in non-test data).
2. **Inherited Cluster Overlap:**
   Phase 5 training inputs share 56 clusters with the held-out test partitions. Unseen held-out clusters total 92 (C_verifier 76 + gold 16).
3. **Small Non-Test Clean Pool:**
   Pre-OOF non-test clean unseen inputs total only 15 clusters. Stop rule S4 requires 44 additional clean clusters from OOF harvest to reach the rule-of-59 threshold.
4. **Logprob Discrepancy under Presence Penalty:**
   In Ollama 0.40.11 (recorded in committed harvest manifests, e.g. sft_A.json), the audit found chosen-token == top-1 on greedy rows at 0.931 (zeroshot) and 0.916 (rft_v2) vs 1.000 for sft/rft, consistent with penalty acting post-logprob, but not proven without running Ollama.

## 7. Held-Out Evaluation Policy

- Held-out partitions (`B1`, `B2`, `C_verifier`, `gold`) are sealed.
- Held-out scoring is permitted ONLY via `ml/train_m2/eval/run_once.py`.
- No model, feature, threshold, or calibrator selection may inspect held-out data.
- Primary evaluation is pre-registered exclusively on the 92 unseen held-out clusters.
